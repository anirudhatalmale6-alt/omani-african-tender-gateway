The trade desk supports Omani suppliers through the whole bid cycle, from deciding whether an opportunity is worth pursuing to assembling the submission.

How the trade desk can help

  • Interpreting eligibility and qualification criteria on a specific tender
  • Certificates of origin, legalisation and documentary requirements
  • Identifying local partners or joint-venture arrangements where a tender requires local content
  • Bid security, bonding and trade finance introductions
  • Clarification questions to the contracting authority

Get in touch

Registered suppliers can raise a request from their dashboard against a specific tender, which routes it to the trade desk with the tender reference already attached.

Contact routing, a ticketed enquiry form and trade-desk case management are part of the full platform build that follows this prototype.

For contracting authorities

African contracting authorities and procurement agencies who would like their notices carried on the gateway are welcome to make contact through the Ministry.
